In this problem set, we explored two real world heat transfer problems. The first shows the impact on energy conservation that thermal insulation has on a water heater. By adding just a 2 cm layer of foam thermal insulation to the water heater, we were able to save 1324.69 Watts of energy. By comparing the cost investment of a one time insulation purchase versus the repeated cost of energy usage for the life of a water heater, this problem makes a clear case for the usage of thermal insulation for these types of units. This is helpful knowledge when it comes to home ownership and individual environmental impact. On a professional level, this knowledge can inform design choices on certain products. The second problem deals with a system including a thin film heater sandwiched between a thermally insulated wall and a plastic layer that is immersed in a coolant. This problem outlines the requirements for maintaining a specific system temperature to avoid overheating. Understanding this type of system will be very helpful as I move through my career in electronics to understand different strategies of avoiding overheating and system failure.
